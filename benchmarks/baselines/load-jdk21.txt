Noise: on the MVEL rows at 100 rules and up (Load mvel 100 and 1000, LoadDense 100 and 500), whose error is 0.6% to
1.5% of the score, a difference under 5% is noise. On Load mvel rules=10 (error 6.3% of the score) and the three noop
rows (9% to 11%), a difference under 15% is. The two steps #1137 found were +15% and +18%, so the general "under
about 15%" rule in benchmarks/README.md, "Reading the numbers", would have passed them; "The load budget" there
owns these thresholds.
